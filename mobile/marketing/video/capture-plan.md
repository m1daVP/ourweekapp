Use a fictional demo household only: Maya and Leo. Suggested content: “Plan Saturday breakfast”, “Book the dentist appointment”, and “Prepare kindergarten clothes in the evening.” Record only a sandbox purchase and a local reminder. Never record a production account, a real payment method, private notes, tokens, or authentication screens.

The required files are `01-home.mp4`, `02-check-in.mp4`, `03-recap.mp4`, `04-history.mp4`, `05-premium.mp4`, `06-reminder.mp4`, `ourweek-tiktok-sunday-conversation.mp4`, `ourweek-tiktok-weekly-ritual.mp4`, and `ourweek-tiktok-nothing-disappears.mp4`. Each source needs a one-second idle pause before the first interaction and after the last interaction so the final edit can breathe.
